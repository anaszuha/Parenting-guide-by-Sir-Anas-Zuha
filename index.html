The Foundation

10 Parenting Principles by Sir Anas Zuha

Wisdom that transforms the parent-child relationship

01

Child Understands Everything

Treat your child as an intelligent human. Behave as if a camera is always recording you — because your child is.

02

No Pre-Conditioning

Do not push the child toward doctor or engineer from day one. Let their interests emerge naturally.

03

Respect = Role Model

Respect your child the way you want them to become. Want a commissioner? Treat them like one.

04

Allow Safe Choices

Don't impose your preferences on harmless decisions. If the child wants a red shirt — let them wear it and learn.

05

Argue, Don't Impose

For harmful choices, hold an argument session. Decide together based on the strongest reason — not parental rank.

06

Build Independence

Let the child handle real tasks — shopping, bargaining, budgeting. Real life is the best teacher.

07

Work as a Family

Today everyone sweeps together. Today everyone washes dishes. Hard work becomes joy when shared.

08

Never Compare Children

Comparison kills confidence. Every child is unique and on their own timeline.

09

No Corruption in Parents

Don't spit on roads, cut queues, or use resources unfairly in front of your child. They are always watching.

10

Pray & Dream Big

Pray for your child daily. Speak big dreams into their life. Your belief in them is their first fuel.
